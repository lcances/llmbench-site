53 of 64 layers fits, and 10.7 tokens/s is what that buys. The card's answer to a 27B is barely yes.

The fifth rung of the Qwen3.5 ladder still does not fit a 16 GB card whole: 15.59 GiB of weights against a 16.00 GiB budget, before a single token of context. But this re-measurement retires rev 1's verdict clause by clause: the fit probe pins 53/64 layers, the best split decodes at 10.7 t/s — 0.7 above the 10 t/s floor — so skipped: null and the full funnel ran: screen, refine, finalists, max-context probes, and ollama alongside. The floor still bites, but its role changed: it now caps context instead of killing the run — every max-context probe stops with capped: decode_floor, the deepest at 16384 tokens. Ollama's runner places the oversized model itself, and beats our 53-layer pin by 11 % at shallow depth (12.2 vs 11.0 t/s at 512, 1.11× through 2k), ties at 8k (0.98×) and falls to 0.54× at 32k (3.6 vs 6.8 t/s) — the 35B's deep-context ollama win does not repeat on a dense model; TTFT at 512 is a wash (1145 vs 1057 ms). Both runtimes' probes stop at 16384, ollama decoding 7.1 t/s there to our 9.7. The 9B on the same card remains the advice — 6.7× faster at 5.5× less energy per token — but it is advice now, not this run's verdict.

READ BEFORE QUOTING — every llama.cpp number here was measured with a disabled kernel. Qwen3.5 is a hybrid architecture and llama.cpp b10068 switches off its fused Gated Delta Net path as soon as any layer sits on the CPU (resolve_fused_ops: … set to disabled, logged in 11 of the 12 fit-trial logs; the 9B's full-offload runs never trip it) — and with the pin at 53/64 that is the whole sweep, not just the probe. So these results are partial offload plus a lost fused kernel, and a build with mixed-placement support could move them. The verdict "this tier barely runs this model" holds for this build, at this quant, today — it is not a claim about the model.

Blocked on data, not on design

What would change this board's verdict, and what it would take.

  • needs a runa smaller quant

    Q3_K_M or IQ3_XXS would fit 16 GB whole — no CPU layers, the fused GDN kernel back, and a context ceiling set by VRAM rather than by the decode floor at 16384. It is a different experiment — quality is out of scope for this harness, so the honest framing is "same model, less precision", not "same model".

  • needs a buildfused Gated Delta Net on mixed placement

    Every number here has the fused GDN kernel disabled because layer 0 is on the CPU. A llama.cpp build that supports the fused path with split placement would re-open the question; re-check on each runtime re-pin.

  • needs hardwarethe second card

    32 GB across two 5060 Ti would hold this model whole with room for context. It is the clearest single argument for the enthusiast tier in the whole series.

  • measured — own boardthe 35B-A3B

    Bigger on disk (20.50 GiB) but a MoE with ~3B active per token, and it now has its own board: expert-aware placement (n_cpu_moe, not whole-layer offload) gives it a verdict this dense 27B could not have predicted.
