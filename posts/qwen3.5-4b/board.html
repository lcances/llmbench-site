At 4B the 16 GB card stops being a detail: flash-attn off runs out of memory at 157696 tokens, on reaches 262144.

Third rung of the Qwen3.5 ladder, same box and the same GGUF file for both runtimes. The first three rungs answered a question about software overhead; this one is the first where the hardware answers back. A 4B with flash-attn off completes a 155648-token context, peaks at 15.85 GB of the card's 15.93, and OOMs at the next rung of the ladder — a measured boundary, not a parameter we chose. Turn flash-attn on and the same card carries 262144 tokens on 11.95 GB. New on this re-measurement: the funnel's own tuned fa-off slot picked an 8192-token batch, and its compute buffers hit the same wall at 30720 tokens — a config, not the card, can also be what says no. Figures run hero-first: the wall, then what a 4B does, then why flash-attn moves it, then the overhead story the earlier rungs opened.

READ BEFORE QUOTING — ollama cannot disable EOS or its prefix cache, so its decode is measured by continuation chaining and every rep unloads and reloads the model. That overhead sits inside its wall time and therefore inside its energy number — treat the J/1k bars as indicative, not as a clean efficiency ranking. TTFT is measured client-side at the first streamed token and load time is spawn → ready, so the two constants this board leans on are unaffected. Probe-matched odd prompt sizes are reps=1 (no spread band). The OOM counts above are results: the max-context probe walks until the card refuses, and where it refuses is the finding.
