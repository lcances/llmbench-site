READ BEFORE QUOTING — ollama cannot disable EOS or its prefix cache, so its decode is measured by continuation chaining and every rep unloads and reloads the model. That overhead sits inside its wall time and therefore inside its energy number — treat the J/1k bars as indicative, not as a clean efficiency ranking. It does not touch the two constants this board leans on: TTFT is measured client-side at the first streamed token, and load time is spawn → ready. Probe-matched odd prompt sizes are reps=1 (no spread band). And two model sizes are two points, not a trend line — the evidence for "the tax is a constant" is the constants themselves, not the slope between them.

Still blocked on data, not on design

  • needs 4B · 9B · 27B · 35B-A3Bthe tax curve with six points

    fig 01 carries the whole series argument on two rungs. The prediction is monotone decay toward a nearly tax-free 27B; four more budget funnels settle it. The meta post's scaling charts also need the 35B labelled as MoE (~3B active) so it is not read as a dense outlier.

  • needs the 27B runthe partial-offload curve

    The fit probe shipped this session but has never met real hardware. 27B is 15.59 GiB against a 16 GB card, so it is the first model whose max context is decided by VRAM rather than by our param ceiling — and where fig 04's fa-off line stops being academic.

  • needs a second cardenthusiast tier columns

    Every number here is single-GPU. The two-tier comparison ("what does the second card buy you") cannot start until the card is installed.

  • needs a bigger modela real power knee

    The 5060 Ti's driver floor is 150 W of 180 and a 2B draws ~160 W under load, so capping is still nearly free. A knee inside the 150–180 W window needs a model that sustains > ~167 W.

  • absent by design · rev 1the mixed-KV fallback trap figure

    The 0.8B board's 17× trap chart has no rows to plot here: uniform-KV-only feasibility means this grid never generates mixed K≠V configs. The canary spread is a boring 1.16× (180–208 t/s) at 156–163 W — every surviving config is GPU-served, which is the rule working as intended.
